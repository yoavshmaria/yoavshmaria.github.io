The topic is in breakout mode, not maturity. Search volume rose from 5,210 in October 2025 to 21,618 in March 2026, a 315% increase in five months, while organic clicks climbed from 2,432 to 8,493 over the same period. Competitor movement has been extremely fast: Semrush went from owning mostly branded AI visibility demand to building a full ecosystem of product pages, help docs, studies, and blog content; Ahrefs and SE Ranking expanded with definitional and tool-led pages; Similarweb has product/help content and a few blog assets, but not yet enough editorial depth to dominate non-branded informational demand. The prioritization logic in this report therefore favors: (1) definition + methodology pages that can win “what is / how to measure” queries, (2) comparison and tool-intent pages where competitors already capture clicks, and (3) citation-ready, research-heavy assets that are structurally attractive for LLMs.

Topic growth signal

Explosive growth. Mar 2026 volume is 2.66x Nov 2025 and 16.6x Apr 2025.

Competitive velocity

Semrush scaled via layered content: overview page, toolkit KB, free checker, studies, methodology, and announcement pages.

Strategic implication

Similarweb should move beyond feature pages and own the category narrative with glossary, methodology, ROI, gap analysis, and comparison content.

What Is AI Visibility? Definition, Metrics, Examples, and How to Improve It

Current URL: https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/what-is-ai-visibility/

Target keywords

Primary: ai visibility (2,820)
Secondary: what is ai visibility (~120), ai search visibility (~700 historical / 220 current click-bearing), llm visibility (330), ai visibility vs seo (~130 inferred), ai brand visibility (brand-adjacent)

Search intent

Primarily informational. Users want a plain-English definition, a framework for measuring the concept, and a clear explanation of how AI visibility differs from rankings, share of voice, and classic SEO.

Content outline

  • H2: What is AI visibility? — one-sentence definition above the fold
  • H2: How AI visibility differs from SEO rankings — compare ranking vs mention/citation presence
  • H2: The 4 metrics that matter — mention share, citation share, prompt coverage, sentiment/accuracy
  • H2: Where AI visibility happens — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews / AI Mode
  • H2: What drives AI visibility — content clarity, entity strength, citations, freshness, authority
  • H2: How to measure it with Similarweb — screenshots + methodology teaser
  • H2: Common mistakes — treating it as rank tracking only
  • H2: FAQ — tool, score, examples, reporting cadence

Why Google ranking

This is the biggest category keyword gap. In the dataset, “ai visibility” has the highest volume (2,820) while Similarweb holds 0% click share in the latest month, despite already having a relevant article. Refreshing the page to become the definitive category page is the fastest way to attack the largest TAM.

Why GenAI / LLM coverage

Definition pages are disproportionately cited by LLMs when they contain explicit definitions, measurable frameworks, FAQ blocks, and sourced stats. Add FAQPage schema, direct-answer intros, original data points, glossary anchors, and clearly labeled definitions.

Estimated traffic opportunity

~350–650 monthly clicks if top-3 across the primary cluster.

Priority

High — biggest non-branded category term and strongest internal-link hub candidate.
